How Ship & Earn works

Everything you need to go from building to earning.

The idea

Every tool, script, template, or app you build is an asset. Each asset can earn money three ways simultaneously — as a product you sell, a gig you offer, and a service you deliver. Ship & Earn calls this the Triple Loop.

The goal: stack assets over time so income compounds. One asset earns a little. Ten assets earn consistently. Twenty assets earn while you sleep.
